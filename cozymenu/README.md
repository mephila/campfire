press `F6` in game to open the menu. drag the title bar to move it, `F6` or `Esc` closes it. click a section title to fold it.

## size

- **custom size**: turns the size on and off
- **size**: 0.2x to 15x, or click a preset
- **keep animation speed**: scales walk animations with size

## name colour

- **one colour**: your name in one colour
- **two colour fade**: your whole name fades back and forth between two colours
- **gradient**: your name shades from the first colour into the second, still or moving
- **rainbow**: your whole name cycles through the rainbow
- **rainbow letters**: each letter is a different rainbow colour, moving along the name
- **chat follows colour**: your chat messages use the name colour too
- **plain name on joins**: keeps your name uncoloured while you join and while other players join

settings are saved in `BepInEx/config/mephila.cozymenu.cfg`.
